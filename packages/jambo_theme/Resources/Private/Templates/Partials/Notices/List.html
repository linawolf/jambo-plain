One list of notices, used by the band on the home page and by the notices page itself. {notices} is what the database-query processor returns: one entry per page, each wrapping the row as .data - hence {notice.data.title} and not {notice.title}. The heading is the link, the abstract of the page is the teaser. The date is printed twice on purpose: once for a machine in the datetime attribute, always ISO and language-independent, and once for a reader with an ICU pattern, which takes the month name from the locale of the current language - "22 Oktoba 2026" in Swahili, where a PHP format string would have said "October" in every language.
  • The heading level is given by the caller, because the same list sits at two depths: under the h2 of the band on the home page, and directly under the h1 on the notices page, where an h3 would skip a level. Fluid cannot build a tag name from a variable, so the two cases are spelled out. The full title, not nav_title: an editor shortens a nav title to fit a menu, and a notice deserves its whole heading here. (An f:or chain would not have helped either - it falls back only on null, and an unset nav_title is an empty string.)
